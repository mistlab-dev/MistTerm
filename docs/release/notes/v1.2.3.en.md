## New

- **Keyboard shortcuts for your saved snippets**: edit a snippet under **Snippets**, click **Record**, press a key combination and save; click **Clear** to remove it. Pressing the shortcut in the terminal works just like clicking the snippet: Mist asks for any variables first, and the command still goes through your team's command check. Shortcuts are stored on this computer only. You can set them on team snippets too; teammates are not affected.
  - On Windows / Linux use **Ctrl+Shift+key** (for example Ctrl+Shift+Y); on Mac use a **⌘** combination (for example ⌘⇧Y).
  - Ctrl+key combinations such as Ctrl+C and Ctrl+R stay with the shell. A single key, Shift+key, Alt/Option combinations (which type characters or move by word in the terminal) and Mist's own shortcuts can't be used; Mist tells you why while you record. It also tells you if another snippet already uses the combination.

## Improvements

- **The Mac icon is now the same size as other apps**: Mist's icon used to look a size bigger than other apps in Launchpad and the Dock. It now has the standard Apple margin, and the Dock icon after launch matches the one in the installer.

## Fixes

- **A deleted snippet kept its shortcut**: when you delete a personal or team snippet (including one a teammate deleted that arrives by sync), its shortcut is removed too and the key combination is free again.
